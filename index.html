Patient Health Record Management System

A simple and secure way to manage patient records.

Nainika • Namita • Nagaraj

The problem

1 Hard to manage: Paper files pile up and get mixed up, so keeping them in order takes a lot of effort.
2 Slow to find: Searching through old files for one patient's details wastes time when it matters most.
3 Easy to lose: Papers can be lost, torn or damaged, and the information is gone for good.
4 Missed appointments: Patients may forget when their next doctor appointment is.
5 Forgotten medicines: Patients may forget which tablet to take and at what time.

Our solution

🗄️ Store: All patient details are kept together in one digital place instead of many paper files.
🔍 Search: Type a name or ID and the record appears quickly, with no digging through files.
🔒 Protect: Only authorized users can log in and see the information they need.
📅 Appointments: The doctor updates the visit date and time, and the patient gets a reminder on that day.
💊 Medicine reminders: Shows which tablet to take and at what time, so no dose is missed.

Main features

🪪 Patient details: Name, age, contact and other basic information.
📋 Medical history: Past illnesses, allergies and treatments in one place.
🗓️ Visit records: Date, symptoms and the doctor's notes for every visit.
🔎 Search & update: Find a patient quickly and keep the record up to date.
🔑 Login & access: Each user signs in and sees only what they need.

Keeping records safe

👥

Login for authorized users

Doctors and staff sign in with their own account.

🛡️

Keep information private

Details are never shown to the public or unrelated people.

🗝️

Allow only required access

Each person sees only the part of the record they need.

☁️

Back up important records

A saved copy protects the data if something goes wrong.

Future improvements

User accounts: Separate doctor and patient logins; patients see own records.
